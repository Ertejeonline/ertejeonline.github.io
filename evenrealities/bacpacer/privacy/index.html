BACpacer for Even G2 privacy policy

Applies to the BACpacer app for Even Realities G2 glasses (com.er.bacpacer). Last updated: 18 November 2026.

In short: BACpacer does not collect, share or sell any data. What you enter stays on your phone.

What is stored

The drinks you log (amount, alcohol % and time), your drink presets, and the settings used for the estimate (weight, height, sex at birth, age or date of birth, metabolism and food). They are stored in the app's local storage inside the Even app on your phone.

What leaves your phone

BACpacer sends no data to the developer or anyone else. It requests no permissions and has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The glasses show what the app draws; the Even app handles the connection between your phone and glasses, and Even Realities ↗ (opens in a new tab)' own privacy policy applies to the Even app.

Deleting your data

Delete single drinks, or all drinks with Reset, on the app's phone page. Removing the app from the Even app removes its stored data.

Children

BACpacer is meant for adults of legal drinking age.

Not a medical device

The blood alcohol figure is a rough estimate from a general formula. It does not measure anything and must never be used to decide whether you can drive or do anything else that needs you to be fully alert.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
