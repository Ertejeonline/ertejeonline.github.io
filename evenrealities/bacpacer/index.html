BACpacer for Even G2

An app for Even Realities G2 smart glasses that helps you keep a steady drinking pace. Log what you drink and see how long to wait for the next one.

Get BACpacer on Even Hub ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Not a medical device. The blood alcohol figure is a rough estimate from a general formula. It does not measure anything. Never use it to decide whether you can drive or do anything else that needs you to be fully alert.

Features

  • Log a drink on the glasses: adjust amount and alcohol %, or pick a saved preset.
  • A countdown to your next drink, right on your glasses.
  • Current and peak blood alcohol estimates with trend arrows, and an estimated time to sober up.
  • Settings for your body, metabolism and food, managed from your phone.
  • Edit or delete logged drinks and manage presets from the phone page.

Privacy

BACpacer needs no permissions and sends nothing anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
