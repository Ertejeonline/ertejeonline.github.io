ER Clock privacy policy

Applies to the ER Clock app for Even Realities G2 glasses (com.er.clock). Last updated: 18 November 2026.

In short: ER Clock does not collect, share or sell any data.

What is stored

Only two settings: where the time is shown and whether seconds are shown. They are stored in the app's local storage inside the Even app on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. ER Clock requests no permissions and has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The time comes from your phone's clock. The Even app handles the connection between your phone and glasses, and Even Realities ↗ (opens in a new tab)' own privacy policy applies to the Even app.

Deleting your data

Removing the app from the Even app removes its stored settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
