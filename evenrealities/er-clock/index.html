ER Clock for Even G2

The current time on your Even Realities G2 glasses, where you want it.

Get ER Clock on Even Hub ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Features

  • Shows the current time and keeps it up to date.
  • Choose the position: top left, top right, center, bottom left or bottom right.
  • Show or hide seconds.
  • Remembers your settings.
  • Double-tap to exit.

Privacy

ER Clock needs no permissions and sends nothing anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
