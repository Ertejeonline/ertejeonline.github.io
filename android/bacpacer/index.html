bacpacer for Android

Log your drinks, pace yourself and see a rough estimate of your blood alcohol over time. Built to be easy to use, even after a few drinks.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Not a medical device. The blood alcohol figure is a rough estimate from a general formula. It does not measure anything. Never use it to decide whether you can drive or do anything else that needs you to be fully alert.

Features

  • Wait at least for: a countdown that paces your drinking at a rate you choose.
  • Estimated peak and current blood alcohol, with a chart for the current session.
  • One tap to log a drink you had before, and undo for every change.
  • Accounts for your body, metabolism and food. Shows ‰ or %.
  • An optional blood alcohol limit and a warning at very high levels.
  • History of past sessions.
  • Wear OS watch app: log drinks and see the timers from your wrist.
  • Large touch targets, TalkBack support and up to 200 % font size.

Privacy

bacpacer asks for no permissions and has no internet access, accounts, ads or tracking. Your data stays on your phone and watch. Read the full privacy policy.
